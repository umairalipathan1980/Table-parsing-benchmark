### **CEO REVIEW**

“Our third quarter was the strongest in the company’s history. Revenue grew by 6.4 per cent to EUR 48.2 million, driven by Site Services, where revenue increased by 9.1 per cent as several large construction projects in the Helsinki region moved into the frame phase. Industrial Supply grew by 5.0 per cent despite softer demand in Sweden. EBIT rose to EUR 4.2 million and the EBIT margin improved by 1.4 percentage points to 8.7 per cent. The improvement came from better pricing discipline, a higher share of service revenue and lower freight costs. Group costs remained at EUR 0.5 million for the quarter. Operating cash flow was EUR 6.1 million, compared with EUR 3.9 million a year earlier, as inventory levels normalised after the spring peak. Our order backlog reached a record EUR 62.5 million at the end of September. Based on the strong development in January–September and the healthy order backlog, we are raising our guidance for 2026. I want to thank our customers and our 412 employees for their commitment.”

*Mika Rantanen, President and CEO*

### **OUTLOOK 2026 (RAISED)**

Kestora expects revenue for 2026 to grow by 5–8 per cent (previously: 3–6 per cent) and the EBIT margin to be 7.0–8.0 per cent (previously: 6.5–7.5 per cent). The outlook assumes no material change in construction activity in Finland during the fourth quarter.

**2025 comparison:**
revenue EUR 178.4 m, EBIT margin 7.0%
