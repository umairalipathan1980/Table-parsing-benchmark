## **1. DEFINITIONS**

1.1 **“Services”** means the cleaning, technical maintenance and security services described in Schedule 1 and priced in Schedule 2. **“Site”** means each property listed in Schedule 1. **“Monthly Fee”** means the fixed monthly fee per Site set out in Schedule 2, excluding VAT.

1.2 **“Business Day”** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Finland. **“Incident”** means any fault or request reported to the Supplier's service desk.

## **2. SCOPE OF SERVICES**

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services at each Site from the start date stated for that Site in Schedule 1, which may differ from the Effective Date.

2.2 Additional work ordered in writing by the Customer is invoiced at the hourly rates in Schedule 2, Part B, subject to the minimum charge stated there.

## **3. TERM AND TERMINATION**

3.1 This Agreement enters into force on **1 July 2026** (the **“Effective Date”**) and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months (the **“Initial Term”**).

## **5. PRICES AND PAYMENT**

5.1 The Customer shall pay the Monthly Fees and hourly charges set out in Schedule 2. All prices are in euros and exclude value added tax, which is added at the rate in force from time to time.

5.2 The Supplier may adjust the prices once per calendar year, with effect from 1 January, by giving sixty (60) days' written notice. The first adjustment may take effect on **1 January 2028**. Any adjustment shall not exceed the change in the official labour cost index for the service sector during the preceding twelve months and in no event four per cent (4 %).

5.3 The Supplier invoices the Monthly Fees in advance on the first Business Day of each month and additional work monthly in arrears. Invoices are payable within **twenty-one (21) days** of the invoice date. Late payments bear interest in accordance with the Finnish Interest Act.

## **6. LIABILITY**

6.1 Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit or business interruption, except in the case of wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

6.2 Each Party's total liability under this Agreement in any contract year is limited to an amount equal to the fees paid or payable by the Customer during the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to liability, but not less than **EUR 100 000**.

## **7. CONFIDENTIALITY**

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party that is marked confidential or should reasonably be understood to be confidential, during the term and for three (3) years thereafter.

## **8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES**

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Finland. Disputes shall first be negotiated in good faith and, failing agreement within thirty (30) days, settled in the Helsinki District Court.

## **12. MISCELLANEOUS**

12.2 In the event of any conflict, the numbered Sections of this Agreement prevail over the Schedules, and both prevail over the Key Terms Summary, which is included for convenience only.
